About the clinic

Good care starts with being listened to

Range Physiotherapy was founded on a simple idea: patients recover better when they are treated as people, not appointments. Our team takes the time to understand how your pain affects your work, your sport and your family — then builds a plan around it.

Every practitioner holds a degree in physiotherapy, is registered with the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency, and completes regular professional development. We are a registered practice with AHSDC.

  1. 01

    Assess

    A 45-minute comprehensive assessment with movement testing, not just a quick look at the sore spot.

  2. 02

    Diagnose & explain

    Plain-language explanation of what is happening and what your realistic timeline looks like.

  3. 03

    Treat & load

    Hands-on treatment plus hands-on-movement: you leave with homework that actually builds progress.

  4. 04

    Strengthen & discharge

    We test objectively, taper visits, and send you off with a maintenance plan. Discharge is the goal.
